Featured Project: Assisto

A verified helper booking platform: book trusted, background-verified local helpers on demand for assist-only, non-medical tasks, from accompanying a parent to a hospital visit to errands for families living away from home.

The Problem

Millions of people in India move to other cities for work, while their parents and family stay behind. When a hospital visit, paperwork or an errand comes up, there is no reliable, verified person to help on short notice. Assisto solves this with on-demand, verified local helpers who can be booked in minutes, with transparent fixed-price plans.

What I Built

  • Three-role marketplace: dedicated Customer, Helper and Admin workflows on Spring Boot REST APIs and a React frontend.
  • Nearby-helper dispatch (Rapido/Uber-style): location-based matching, round-based job offers with TTL, and first-accept-wins assignment so one job is never double-assigned.
  • Security: JWT authentication with refresh-token rotation, role-based access control (RBAC), account lockout, and a helper verification flow before anyone can accept jobs.
  • Booking lifecycle: status management with PIN-verified job start/end, pricing, payment workflows and full audit tracking of every state change.
  • Pricing: fixed-price plans plus travel cost and GST handling, designed to vary by region.
  • Bilingual UI: English and Hindi so the product is usable by its Indian audience.
  • Production-ready: Dockerized PostgreSQL, backend and frontend services with Flyway database migrations, hosted on DigitalOcean.
  • CI/CD: GitHub Actions pipeline that auto-deploys every push to the production server.

How a Booking Works

  1. 1Customer books a helper, choosing a plan and location.
  2. 2Dispatch engine finds the nearest verified helpers and sends round-based offers with a time-to-live.
  3. 3First helper to accept wins; the others' offers expire automatically.
  4. 4PIN-verified start confirms the right helper has arrived; the job is tracked through its lifecycle.
  5. 5PIN-verified end, then payment and an audit trail of the whole booking.
